A Supplier That Answers Like an Owner

KABER (SHENZHEN) TRADING CO., LTD. is a Shenzhen-based export house for bicycle suspension seatposts — direct access to the decision-maker, independent quality control on every order, and a track record of repeat buyers.

Who We Are

KABER (SHENZHEN) TRADING CO., LTD. is an export company based in the Shenzhen Special Economic Zone, backed by a parent group with over a decade of manufacturing and cross-border trade experience. Our core line is bicycle suspension seatposts — iron and aluminum, straight and flippable, from 25.4 mm to 33.9 mm in diameter and 300 mm to 500 mm in length — supplied to OEM/ODM buyers across Europe, the Americas and Asia.

Because we work between you and a network of vetted factories, you get factory-level pricing without factory-level friction: one accountable team handles your quote, samples, production follow-up and shipment — no hand-offs, no waiting for approvals, no lost messages.
