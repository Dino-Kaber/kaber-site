Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers for buyers — and if you do not see your question here, ask us directly.

Customization first: all specifications and packaging can be adjusted to your needs — send us your requirements for a tailored quote.

What diameters do you offer?

25.4, 27.2, 28.6, 31.8 and 33.9 mm — covering most seat tube standards on the market.

What lengths are available?

300 mm, 400 mm and 500 mm, depending on the model.

What types are available?

Straight and Flippable. Flippable models carry an adjustable lever that flips the setup in seconds; elasticity is tuned via the collar at the base. Straight models have a fixed structure.

Can I adjust the elasticity?

Yes on the adjustable models — and elasticity can be customized to your target rider weight and terrain. The 28.6 mm iron straight model is preset at the factory by default.

Is the 28.6 mm Flippable adjustable?

Not as standard — the 28.6 mm straight model is preset at the factory. However, every size, including 28.6 mm, can be produced as a flippable, adjustable version on request. All sizes are fully customizable.

Which models include a bushing?

The 31.8 mm and 33.9 mm versions.

What material is the 31.8 mm version?

Aluminum alloy. The 33.9 mm version is also aluminum; the 25.4 / 27.2 / 28.6 mm models are iron.

What is the max load?

100 kg (220 lb). We do not recommend use above 100 kg.

What colors are available?

Matte black and silver as standard. Custom finishes are available on request.

How are the products packaged?

Flexibly — bulk packing for large orders, individual cartons, wider cartons or woven bags. Pieces per carton adjust to your order; tell us your preference and we will confirm the packing plan.

What is your MOQ and lead time?

Both depend on the model, quantity and customization. Send your inquiry and we will reply with MOQ, lead time and the best price.
