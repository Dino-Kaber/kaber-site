Complete Suspension Seatpost Range

Eight models covering 25.4 to 33.9 mm seat tubes, 300 to 500 mm lengths, in iron and aluminum alloy. All dimensions shown in both metric and imperial units — straight to the point, no guesswork.

Straight Type

Fixed elastic structure. The 28.6 mm iron straight model is preset at the factory; other straight models offer adjustable elasticity. Simple, robust and budget-friendly.

Flippable Type

Flippable lever design — one simple flip changes the setup, no tools needed. Elasticity is tuned via the collar at the base (adjustable on most models). The most popular option for e-bikes and mountain bikes.
