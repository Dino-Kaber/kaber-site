Packaging That Fits Your Order

Packaging is decided by order quantity and your requirements — not fixed per model. Bulk packing maximizes container utilization; carton counts adjust to your preference.

Standard Options

Bulk Packing

Loose-packed for maximum container capacity — the typical choice for large orders. Around 50 pieces per carton for the standard 25.4 / 27.2 / 28.6 mm models.

Individual Carton

Each seatpost in its own carton for retail-ready presentation — around 20 pieces per master carton.

Custom Per Order

Wider cartons for the 31.8 mm model, tightly wrapped woven bags for the 33.9 mm model, or any combination you specify. Pieces per carton are always adjustable.

How it works: tell us your quantity and packaging preference in the inquiry. We confirm the packing plan, carton dimensions and container loading calculation with your order quotation.
